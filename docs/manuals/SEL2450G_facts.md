# Sony FE 24-50mm F2.8 G (SEL2450G) — verified facts

The official operating-instructions PDF (≈1.5 MB pictogram leaflet, 2024-02-25) is on Sony support
(https://www.sony.ca/en/electronics/support/lenses-e-mount-lenses/sel2450g/manuals) but blocks automated download.
Download it manually into this folder if wanted. Facts below are cross-checked from Sony spec pages and hands-on reviews.

## Layout (front → mount)
1. Filter thread 67 mm (non-rotating), hood bayonet (ALC-SH178 petal hood, reversible, no lock button)
2. Focus ring — ridged/rubberised, at the front; linear-response MF, ~140–180° throw MFD→∞ (reviews differ)
3. Zoom ring — middle, markings 24 / 28 / 35 / 50; ~80° rotation; barrel extends ~2 cm toward 24 mm (shortest at 50 mm)
4. Aperture ring — near the mount; A + F2.8 … F22 in 1/3 stops
Left side: AF/MF focus-mode switch (flush) + round Focus Hold button (assignable from camera Custom Key settings)
Bottom: Aperture Click ON/OFF switch (de-click for video)
No iris lock (Phillip Reeve review; some retailer pages wrongly list one). Metal mount with rubber gasket, dust & moisture resistant.

## Specs
- 24–50 mm, F2.8 constant, min F22, 11 circular blades
- 16 elements / 13 groups: 4 aspherical, 2 ED
- MFD AF 0.19 m (24 mm) / 0.30 m (50 mm); MF 0.18 / 0.29 m; max magnification 0.30× AF, 0.33× MF
- Ø74.8 × 92.3 mm, 440 g; no OSS (uses body IBIS); internal focusing; not parfocal
- AF: linear motors, fast and quiet; supports up to 120 fps AF/AE tracking on compatible bodies

## Focus Hold behaviour (Sony G-lens manuals)
Press during AF → AF stops, focus fixed, shutter can be released. Release while half-pressing → AF resumes.
